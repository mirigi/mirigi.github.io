Mirigi Building Setup

Tell us how your building works. Start with the basics and return whenever you have more details. Your progress is saved on this device and backed up each time you press Save progress.

BasicsOperationsServicesModules
01 / START HERE

Building basics

These details identify the building and establish the structure residents will see in Mirigi.

02 / PEOPLE AND PLACES

Staff and residents

Add the people who manage the building. Residents can be added now or imported later with unit, name, email, phone number, and language.

Structure and technology

Describe floors, towers, elevator zones, home automation, and the access control system so we can configure the right connections.

Packages and deliveries

Tell us where deliveries arrive and how staff should record the handoff to a resident.

03 / DAILY SERVICES

Reservations

Add one card for each amenity. You can describe the schedule and booking limits in plain language.

Requests and workflows

Create a workflow for each request type. Include the escalation order and, when useful, an AI triage prompt describing who handles each kind of request.

04 / OPTIONAL MODULES

Phonebook and optional modules

Select what you want to activate. Configuration can be completed progressively as the building ramps up.

Panic button

Who receives fire, security, and medical alerts? Include emergency contact rules.

Guest authorization

Describe concierge coverage, visitor ID or photo requirements, and whether an entrance intercom or door station can be answered by Mirigi to authorize guests on its own.

Building cameras

List cameras, brands, RTSP details, and which are resident or staff only.

Restaurant / food ordering

List the restaurants, bars, or cafés that will take orders, plus menus, schedules, ordering options, and restaurant staff.

Valet parking / vehicles

Describe parking zones, valets, car wash pricing, vehicle delivery times, and where vehicles are received and delivered.

Automatically log inbound and outbound cars?

Building events

Define event languages, registration, guest limits, and paid events.

Polls / surveys

Describe target groups and whether results are public or staff-only.

Accounting / fees

Provide the accounting system, currency, and unit mapping requirements.

Reports

Mirigi comes with prebuilt reports for each module. Describe any specific report you would like created for your setup, and who should receive it.

Digital signatures

List signature pads or tablets, their locations, and email receipt preferences.

AI generation and translation

This sets how Mirigi writes and translates text for the building, such as event and poll descriptions, menus, and notices, so the same content is available in every language the building needs. List the languages required.

WhatsApp access

Residents reach the building through Miri, the Mirigi assistant, in the WhatsApp they already use. Confirm chat, image, and voice input.

Temporary stays

Allow residents to invite guests or temporary occupants for a fixed period from the app, through Miri text messages, or both.

AI assistant for staff

Help staff handle complex processes across the system using voice, chat, or both.

Please do not type passwords, PINs or access keys anywhere in this form. We will ask for credentials separately and securely. What you enter is stored in a private Mirigi spreadsheet and used only to set up your building.
