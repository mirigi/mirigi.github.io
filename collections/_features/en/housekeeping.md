Good housekeeping respects the resident’s time and privacy. Mirigi makes it easy to request a cleaning or arrange a regular service, with **hotel-style or scheduled housekeeping** to suit the property.

Managers plan the team’s work from the staff console. Housekeepers stay connected from their phones and report by voice or text, so the reception team spends less time chasing updates.

Residents can also ask Miri: *“Book housekeeping every Friday.”* A familiar routine, arranged through a simple conversation.
