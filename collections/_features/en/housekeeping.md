Keep every home and shared space **ready for the people who use it**. Mirigi brings housekeeping requests, recurring cleaning schedules and the team’s daily work into one place, so residents know how to ask for service and staff know **what needs doing, where and when**.

**Two modes, tailored to the property.** In **hotel-style mode**, residents use a simple **Do Not Disturb / housekeeping switch** to signal whether they want privacy or cleaning service. In **scheduler mode**, they arrange cleaning for a chosen day and available time window, including **recurring bookings**. A regular Friday cleaning becomes a routine the building can plan around.

**Assisted planning for the team, in either mode.** Managers organize the day from the **staff console**, assign cleaning tasks and review each housekeeper’s schedule. Availability and scheduling checks help them identify conflicts before assigning work. Staff have their own **daily route and task details**, while managers can follow progress and adjust assignments as the day unfolds.

**Report from wherever the work happens.** Housekeepers update task progress from their phone and add **text reports or voice-dictated notes** while moving between units and shared spaces. Completion notes and issues stay with the task, giving the next shift and management a clear record without a trip back to the front desk.

**A recurring booking can start with a conversation.** Ask Miri, *“Book housekeeping every Friday.”* She helps gather the time window and start date needed to arrange the recurring service, so residents can set up their routine in everyday language.
