Mirigi wasn't built only for condominiums: the same platform runs naturally in **hotels and hospitality properties**. Every guest, from a walk-in to a returning member, reaches **Miri** the moment they check in, through the **branded app** or straight on **WhatsApp**, with nothing to install.

Guests ask for what they need in plain language: **housekeeping, room service, a late checkout, a spa reservation, or a question about the property**, and Miri carries the request through to completion, exactly as she does for residents. There's no learning curve and no separate hotel app to download: it's the same conversation people already have open.

Behind the scenes, each guest's access is **scoped to their stay**, opening automatically at check-in and closing at check-out, with no lingering access afterward. For the **front desk and hotel staff**, every guest request flows into the same **operational console** used across the property: nothing new to learn, just guests to take care of, and fewer calls tying up the front desk during peak hours.
