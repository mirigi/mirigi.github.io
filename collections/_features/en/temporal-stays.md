Renting out your unit on **Airbnb, Booking.com, or any short-term platform**? **Temporary Stays** let owners register a guest's visit and open the building's services to them for exactly the length of their stay: no permanent account, no lingering access after checkout.

Once a temporary stay is registered, the guest receives a **text message or email** with everything they need to get started. From there, they can reach the building the same way residents do: through the **branded app**, or through **Miri on WhatsApp** for guests who'd rather not install anything. Either way, they can **request services from staff, submit maintenance requests, or book amenities** (a massage, the pool, the gym), just like any resident would.

For owners and management, access is **scoped to the stay's dates**: it opens automatically when the stay begins and closes when it ends, so there's never a manual step to remember and never lingering access once the guest has checked out. For staff, a temporary guest shows up in the same **operational workflows** as any resident request: nothing new to learn, just a guest to take care of.
