Not everyone wants to download another app. **Miri on WhatsApp** brings the same AI concierge that acts, not just answers, into a conversation people already have open every day. No install, no account setup, no learning a new interface — just message Miri like any other contact and she takes it from there.

Everything Miri can do in the app, she can do on WhatsApp: **order from the restaurant, call the valet, book amenities, submit a service request, or ask a question about the building.** The conversation is the interface — residents, guests, and temporary visitors type naturally, and Miri carries the request through to completion, exactly as she would through the branded app.

This makes Miri the fastest possible front door for anyone who shouldn't need a full account to get help — a guest staying for a weekend, a visiting family member, or anyone who simply prefers messaging over apps. It's the same trusted concierge, reachable wherever people already are.
